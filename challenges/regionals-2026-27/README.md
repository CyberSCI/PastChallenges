## Backstory

> Life is peaceful in the technologically advanced lakeside beaver colony - until a powerful AI company, Machinic Corp., sets its sights on their home.
> 
> The AI has plans for the habitat. It wants to clear the forest to make space for an enormous data center and divert the lake’s water to cool its servers. The beavers stand in its way.
> 
> To drive them out, the AI unleashes hordes of agents against the colony’s computer systems. The cyberattacks have begun, and the beavers need your help.
> 
> Can you defend the colony before it loses its home?
