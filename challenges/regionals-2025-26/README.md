## Backstory

> The year is 2025. A giant meteor explodes in Earth's atmosphere, and the resulting electromagnetic pulse destroys all modern electronic devices. Humanity is thrown back into the Stone Age. 
> 
> Despair sets in. But not everyone has given up hope. Engineers scour basements, warehouses, and museums in search of old electronics, with the goal of restoring the world's computing and communication capabilities. We are trying to update and secure legacy devices and software to make them usable again.
>  
> We need all the help we can get. Please join us in our mission to save our civilization!
