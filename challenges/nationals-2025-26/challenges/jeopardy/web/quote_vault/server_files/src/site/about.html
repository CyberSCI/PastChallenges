About this archive

Posted by Admin on Aug 3, AS 3  ·  Edited Mar 11, AS 7.

QuoteVault is a small, hand-curated archive of pre-Strike literature. We started it three winters after the impact, once Block 7 had light again and someone had the patience to scan the books that survived the Hollow Library fire. It's a quiet corner of the public net, hosted off a salvaged tower from the old times.

The collection

We began with ten of our favorites — Einstein, Wilde, Twain, Edison, and a handful of others — recovered word by word from photographs of damaged pages. We add more as the foragers bring them in. Browse the archive for what speaks to you, or pull a card off the daily print run.

Status

This is an early-cycle build. Things shift around as we rewire.

About

QuoteVault is maintained by the Block 7 archive team. We meet on Thursdays in the boiler room.
